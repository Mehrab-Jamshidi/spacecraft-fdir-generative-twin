# Declaration on the Use of AI Tools

*This is the Markdown version of Appendix D of the thesis, reproduced here so that the
declaration travels with the code.*

In the interest of transparency, this section declares the use of artificial-intelligence
tools in the preparation of this thesis. The tools used were the large-language-model
assistants ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic). Their use was discussed with my
supervisor, and the assistance they provided is disclosed here rather than cited, as it does
not constitute a citable source.

**Scope.** AI tools were used as an assistive aid for writing, programming and analysis. The
research objectives, the experimental design, the choice and preparation of the dataset, the
training and execution of every model and experiment, and the scientific conclusions are my
own; AI was used to support and accelerate that work, not to replace the underlying research.

**Text drafting and editing.** AI tools were used to help draft and restructure prose, to
improve clarity and English expression, and to prepare tables and figure captions. All text
was reviewed, revised where necessary and approved by me, and reflects my own understanding
of the work.

**Code generation and debugging.** AI tools assisted in writing and debugging the analysis,
data-processing, statistical and plotting code, and in producing figures from my own results.
The implementation choices were mine, and I checked and validated the behaviour of the
resulting code.

**Result interpretation.** AI tools were used as a discussion and sanity-checking aid when
reasoning about results — for example, to help articulate findings, weigh alternative
explanations and identify appropriate analyses. The final interpretation, the framing of every
claim and the scientific conclusions are entirely my own.

**Responsibility and verification.** I conceived the research objectives, made or approved
every methodological decision, and verified the reported results against the underlying data.
I understand the methods, the data and the results in full, I am able to explain and defend
the entire content of this thesis, and I take full responsibility for its scientific validity
and for any errors it may contain.

— Mehrab Jamshidi
